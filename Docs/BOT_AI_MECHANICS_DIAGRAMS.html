How UE1 bots work in SurrealEngine

UT436 Botpack.Bot and Unreal Gold 226b UnrealShare.Bots use stock UnrealScript for decisions and native UE1 services for pathfinding, movement, collision, and callbacks.

The diagrams show the actual compatibility boundary. An enhanced policy is optional; stock script remains the authority for normal bot intent.
